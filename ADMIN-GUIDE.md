# Editing the website

Greg, this is how to add and change projects, manufacturers and presentations on fuchs-sales.ca yourself.

## Signing in
Go to **https://www.fuchs-sales.ca/admin** and sign in with your email and password. Forgot your password? Ask Matt to set a new one. After 5 wrong tries, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.

You'll see three boxes: **Projects**, **Manufacturers** and **Presentations**. Each has **Add** and **Change or remove**.

Nothing changes on the website until you press the black button at the bottom of the screen (**Save changes**, or **Add … to website** for something new). After you save, the website updates by itself in **about 2 minutes**. Every change is kept, so a mistake can always be undone. Just ask Matt.

## Add a project
1. **Projects → Add a project**.
2. **About the project**: the name, a short description (e.g. *Tree Pits*), the city and province, the year and a few sentences about the job. The web page address is made from the name for you.
3. **Main photo**: drag a photo onto the box, or tap it to choose one. This is the big photo at the top of the project page.
4. **More photos**: drag in as many as you like (you can pick several at once). Add a caption to each. Use the arrows to change the order.
   - **Step-by-step build** (like Cloverdale): add **4 or more** photos and give **every** photo a **Step name** (e.g. *Excavation*, *Liner*, *Finished*), in build order. A green tick tells you when it's set up.
5. **Products used**: tick the manufacturers whose products were used.
6. **Type of work**: tap one you've used before, or type a new one and press **Add**. These are the filter buttons on the Projects page.
7. Press **Add project to website**.

If something is missing, it's marked in red with a note saying what to fix.

## Add a manufacturer
1. **Manufacturers → Add a manufacturer**.
2. Company name, website, a one-line description and (optionally) what makes them stand out.
3. **Logo**: a PNG with a see-through background looks best. If the logo is white, choose the **Dark** background.
4. **Product categories**: tap the ones that fit.
5. **Documents**: drag in the PDFs (data sheets, brochures…). Check each document's name and kind. They show on the Resources page in this order.
6. Press **Add manufacturer to website**.

## Add a presentation
**Presentations → Add a presentation**: title, an optional second line, the manufacturer, a description, topics and a picture. For diagrams or logos choose **Show the whole picture**; for photos, **Fill the space**.

## Changing or removing
Open the list, tap the item, change what you need and press **Save changes**. To take something off the website, scroll to the bottom and press **Remove**. You'll be asked to confirm.

## Tips
- Phone photos are fine: they're made smaller automatically before uploading.
- If you leave a page with unsaved changes, you'll be asked first.
- Press **Sign out** (top right) when you're done on a shared computer.
